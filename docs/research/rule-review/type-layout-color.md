# Review of typography, layout, and color rules

This review covers 36 catalog IDs. For each, the review compared the short rule and both captions with its four authored deep-dive sections and the implementation of its example. The color hover example lives in the motion showcase. The other 35 examples live in the three category preview modules.

The source column identifies what establishes a requirement, explains browser behavior, or supplies a precedent. A system’s type scale is evidence of a working design choice within that system. Neither that scale nor a static preview establishes a reading-speed, discomfort, or conversion effect for Axiom’s audience.

## Typography

| Rule | Evidence and editorial decision | What the example establishes and how to challenge it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| typo-1 | Treat sentence case as an English editorial convention. Retain proper names, initialisms, and localized strings exactly as authored. Microsoft explicitly distinguishes ordinary labels from titles that require their official casing. [Microsoft capitalization](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/capitalization) | The invite dialog changes the case of its title and actions. It illustrates consistency. It cannot establish that title case hurts reading speed. Try an official mixed-case product name and a German noun before adopting any automated transform. |
| typo-2 | Positive tracking can help compact uppercase Latin labels. The amount depends on the face and size. USWDS varies its recommended tracking with the treatment. WCAG’s spacing values test the user’s ability to override spacing. [USWDS typography](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/typography/) · [WCAG text spacing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/text-spacing.html) | Compare the same long uppercase nav label at 0 and 0.05em. Long strings accumulate appreciably more extra width. Check localization and clipping before applying the token broadly. |
| typo-3 | Treat 1.5 as a body-leading starting point. The WCAG AA criterion requires content to survive specified user overrides, including a 1.5 line-height override. It sets no global authoring minimum for paragraph leading. [WCAG text spacing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/text-spacing.html) · [USWDS line height](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/typesetting/line-height/) | The two 16px paragraphs use 18px and 24px leading. Assess repeated lines with real accents and descenders at both narrow and wide measures. Remove the unsupported assertion that tighter leading suits wider columns. |
| typo-4 | Distinguish reading a paragraph from grouping a short heading. Carbon publishes different line heights for individual type roles and sizes. It supplies a precedent for role-specific leading, without establishing a universal heading ratio. [Carbon type sets](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/typography/type-sets) | The same 22px title wraps at 24px versus 33px leading. Check a translated, accented heading before making the tighter token a default. |
| typo-5 | Select families for jobs. USWDS models separate family roles and documents the faces assigned to them. The catalog’s two-family maximum and prohibition on pairing two sans faces have no basis in that guidance. [USWDS font families](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/typesetting/font-family/) | The landing card compares one family with two similar sans stacks. The stacks include platform fallbacks. Inspect the rendered fonts for the target script and judge whether the role distinction is useful. |
| typo-6 | Essential secondary copy still needs the applicable text contrast. Keep the employee’s email readable when it disambiguates a name. Remove the claim that less contrast has zero legibility cost. [WCAG contrast minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) | The member row changes size **and** color. It illustrates two complete treatments and cannot isolate the effect of either. Compare two people with the same display name and check their email text on its actual surface. |
| typo-7 | Comparable amounts benefit from a shared edge and consistent formatting. Tabular figures give digits equal advances when the font provides them. They cannot align decimal marks with different numbers of fractional digits on their own. [GOV.UK numeric columns](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/table/) · [CSS numeric glyph variants](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-fonts-4/#font-variant-numeric-prop) | Both order summaries already align right and use equal fractional precision. Vary digits containing 1 and 8 to inspect the advance widths. Check credits, unit suffixes, and varying precision separately. |
| typo-8 | Start-align sustained prose to the language’s reading direction. GOV.UK explicitly calls for right alignment in right-to-left translations. Centering can suit brief self-contained text. [GOV.UK alignment guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/font-override-classes/#text-alignment) | The English changelog body shares a fixed leading edge in one pane. Both retain the same heading. The equivalent Arabic layout needs its anchor on the opposite side. |
| typo-9 | Tie weight changes to semantic roles. Carbon combines weights, sizes, and leading in named styles. The digest’s two-weight solution supports this particular hierarchy and supplies no ceiling for an entire design system. [Carbon type sets](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/typography/type-sets) | The four-weight example also promotes metadata to bold. Judge which line dominates. Counting distinct weights alone would miss that error. |
| typo-10 | Ellipsis indicates clipped content and preserves a column. It supplies no interaction for obtaining the full text. A person choosing between nearly identical files needs access through keyboard and touch. [CSS overflow and ellipsis](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-overflow-3/#text-overflow) · [WCAG reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) | The long filename gains a single-line ellipsis in the bounded flex item. The static panes prove only the layout difference. A production file picker also needs a full-name path before a consequential choice. |
| typo-11 | Choose body size using actual glyphs and viewing conditions. CSS pixels alone do not capture x-height. WCAG requires text resize support with defined exceptions. It specifies no 14px mobile or 12px desktop floor. [USWDS optical sizing](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/typesetting/overview/) · [WCAG resize text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html) | The alert compares 16px/12px with 11px/9px. Enlarge to 200% and inspect the secondary security detail. Choosing a body token still requires representative readers and devices. |
| typo-12 | Balanced wrap requests more even lines and leaves precise breaks to the engine. Prefer it where the heading reads well across its breaks. [CSS text wrapping](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-text-4/#text-wrap-style) | A 19ch heading deliberately elicits a different break. Check a proper name, a number and unit, or a different font before generalizing the result. |
| typo-13 | Pretty wrapping allows the engine to improve paragraph typography. WebKit documents a paragraph-wide approach and different choices by other engines. Remove the assertion that it only moves the last word. [WebKit implementation](https://webkit.org/blog/16547/better-typography-with-text-wrap-pretty/) · [CSS text wrapping](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-text-4/#text-wrap-style) | The 23ch card description was chosen to expose a short last line. No exact final word or line count is guaranteed. Cross-engine comparison is especially relevant here. |

## Layout and spacing

| Rule | Evidence and editorial decision | What the example establishes and how to challenge it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| layout-1 | Use a named scale for recurring spatial relationships. USWDS includes half and small pixel steps alongside its main system, a concrete counterexample to universal divisibility by four. Remove the fractional-device-pixel guarantee. [USWDS spacing units](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/spacing-units/) | The settings panel labels 16px/8px versus 13px/5px. Show what repeated components gain from the established vocabulary. Special-case optical offsets and hairlines can remain. |
| layout-2 | Limit sustained prose to a useful measure. USWDS gives a range of 45–90 characters and a target near 66 for long reading. The CSS ch unit is a font metric based on the zero glyph. [USWDS measure](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/typography/) · [CSS font-relative lengths](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-values-4/#ch) | The simulated page is 95ch and the capped paragraph is 45ch. Neither width promises that many characters in proportional type. Check real lines, font fallback, language, and enlarged text. |
| layout-3 | Use space to show label–field ownership. In the DOM, associate labels with their inputs and group related controls where appropriate. [WAI grouping controls](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/grouping/) · [Carbon spacing](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/spacing/overview) | The diagram compares 8px/24px with equal 16px gaps. It illustrates visual association. Real controls with hint and error text still need a markup check. |
| layout-4 | Account for optical margins in asymmetric artwork. Apple explicitly includes these margins in the design of symbol assets. Check the supplied SVG before adding a second adjustment. [Apple custom symbols](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2021/10250/) | The 18px play artwork shifts 2px inside a stationary hit area. Its rounded path, internal padding, and orientation matter. No centroid formula gives a universal 2px correction. |
| layout-5 | Size a button for its label and task. USWDS describes a minimum target and responsive handling, and WCAG 2.5.8 defines its own size-or-spacing test with exceptions. Neither rule establishes a fixed padding ratio. [USWDS button](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/button/) · [WCAG target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) | The only varied dimension is inline padding, 8px versus 16px, with 8px block padding. Check full hit area and the longest translated label separately. |
| layout-6 | Relate the symbol to its first line when the supporting copy wraps. Flex start aligns boxes. Apple also shows how artwork margins affect typographic alignment. [CSS box alignment](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-align-3/#align-items-property) · [Apple symbol alignment](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2021/10250/) | The actual bell combines 13px artwork, start alignment, and a 1px top margin. Call this first-line anchoring. The implementation measures no cap height. Check long detail and large type. |
| layout-7 | A card is appropriate for an independent content unit. Sequential content can use headings and spacing. USWDS gives specific cases for choosing a card or a simpler structure. [USWDS card guidance](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/card/) | One stat panel has four boundaries and the other uses a distinct surface. Remove a boundary and ask whether a reader loses an action or grouping, especially when cards sit beside each other. |
| layout-8 | Use consistent spacing to express equivalent relationships. CSS defines independent column and row gutters. A larger row gap can intentionally denote horizontal groups. [CSS gutters](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-align-3/#gaps) · [Carbon spacing](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/spacing/overview) | The two-by-two diagram compares 8px/8px and 8px/24px. Distinguish an unlabeled grid of peers from a grid organized in pairs. Both patterns have legitimate uses. |
| layout-9 | Change outer insets when content needs the space. GOV.UK defines both static and responsive spacing scales. A stable internal gap is an intentional option. [GOV.UK spacing](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/spacing/) | The simulated phone reduces 24px to 12px on each side, recovering 24px overall. The example addresses card padding and supplies no reason to scale every margin or shrink targets. |
| layout-10 | Assign spaces to levels of grouping. Carbon distinguishes uses within its spacing scale. Constant increments and intermediate tokens can be useful. [Carbon spacing](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/spacing/overview) | The skeleton compares 8/16/32 with 8/13/18. The larger final section break explains the difference. No evidence establishes that readers count multiples of the smallest space. |
| layout-11 | A stacking context determines which z-index values compete. Native top-layer entries have separate paint ordering. [CSS stacking contexts](https://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/visuren.html#z-index) · [CSS top layer](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-position-4/#top-layer) | Both diagrams put the modal above the dropdown despite different numbers. Use that equivalence to teach system maintainability. A real transformed ancestor or a native dialog is needed to demonstrate escaping a context. |

## Color and depth

| Rule | Evidence and editorial decision | What the example establishes and how to challenge it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| color-1 | Assign color by role. Atlassian distinguishes primary, neutral, warning, and danger roles. Its guidance supports purposeful accents. A percentage of painted area establishes neither reading order nor conversion. [Atlassian color roles](https://atlassian.design/foundations/color) | New report competes with a blue header, sidebar, and Upgrade chip. The pane labels **where** the blue appears. Retain 60-30-10 as a discoverable heuristic and remove any requirement to audit pixels. |
| color-2 | Black on white is 21:1 and the near-black pair is about 17.9:1. Both exceed the enhanced criterion for ordinary text. The formula measures luminance contrast. It has no output for eye strain. [WCAG enhanced contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-enhanced.html) | The fixed white reading card now compares its declared ink token with an unrelated literal. Preserve both measured ratios, remove the glare verdict, and respect users who prefer maximum contrast. |
| color-3 | A shadow has a color, offsets, blur, and spread. Atlassian pairs selected shadows with surface roles. A branded glow is one art direction. The physical claim that shadows universally inherit an object’s color is unnecessary. [CSS outer shadows](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#shadow-layers) · [Atlassian elevation](https://atlassian.design/foundations/elevation) | The original prose quoted 20% while the implementation changed tint and alpha together. Both panes now use the same geometry and alphas of 0.45 and 0.30, with a named campaign tint to explain the choice. |
| color-4 | Status meaning depends on the product, culture, words, and symbols. WCAG requires information to be available through a cue in addition to color. Atlassian’s role names supply a model for product semantics. [WCAG use of color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html) · [Atlassian color roles](https://atlassian.design/foundations/color) | The same feed toolbar contains Like and Delete. Their words and shapes already distinguish them. Frame the shared red as a local emphasis choice and remove the prediction of increased misclicks. |
| color-5 | A purely decorative divider is outside the non-text contrast test. A required control indicator or meaningful graphical part has a different test. [WCAG non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) | The read-only settings rows have labels and values even with lines removed. Neutral-200 versus neutral-400 can be judged for hierarchy. Reusing the winner for an empty field would change the question. |
| color-6 | WCAG 1.4.11 sets 3:1 against adjacent colors for required visual information. WAI explicitly distinguishes controls that can be identified through other sufficiently contrasting content. Relative darkness compared with a divider is insufficient. [WAI control boundaries and adjacent colors](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html#boundaries) | The old neutral-300 edge on white was around 1.5:1. The revised neutral-500 edge is about 4.73:1 on white. The neutral-400 edge is about 6.91:1 against the dark neutral-900 field fill, computed from the installed achromatic OKLCH tokens. Keep the divider at its previous value and verify both actual adjacent surfaces if the stage changes. |
| color-7 | Assign hover a role separate from success, danger, and persistent selection. WCAG also requires identified components and meaningful states to remain distinguishable. There is no WCAG same-hue requirement for hover. [Atlassian color roles](https://atlassian.design/foundations/color) · [WCAG non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) | The showcase transitions from blue to darker blue or to green, then reverses on exit. Explain possible confusion **if** green indicates completed work nearby. Try keyboard and touch before relying on hover. |
| color-8 | Design semantic pairs in each theme. Atlassian uses lighter raised dark surfaces together with shadows. This is one working approach. A blanket desaturation step cannot guarantee appropriate contrast. [Atlassian elevation in dark themes](https://atlassian.design/foundations/elevation) | The simulated dark storage card compares a tuned palette against an inverted palette with a negative thumbnail. The accent’s 91%→55% figures describe HSL saturation in this illustration. They are not a recipe for other brands. |
| color-9 | For ordinary text at AA, the threshold is 4.5:1. Large text can use 3:1. Large means at least 18pt regular or 14pt bold, with equivalent sizes for CJK, and exceptions exist. Use the unrounded ratio when determining pass or fail. [WCAG contrast minimum and rationale](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) | The helper text uses a fixed white background so the ratios remain reproducible in either theme. The values round to 4.7:1 and 2.3:1. No extrapolation about who can read a given sample follows from an AA pass. |
| color-10 | A translucent foreground composites with its backdrop. Therefore a derived tint can adapt to different surfaces while producing different contrast on each. Opaque semantic tokens remain valid. [W3C simple alpha compositing](https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/#simplealphacompositing) · [Atlassian surface and neutral tokens](https://atlassian.design/foundations/elevation) | The simulated nav uses one blue source for hover and selected fills. The literal pastel examples remain light in dark mode. Check whether the selected state has a persistent, distinguishable indicator. |
| color-11 | Inset image outlines are a framing technique. Alpha compositing explains why a dark line on a dark crop contributes little. No source here requires all photographs to have borders. [W3C alpha compositing](https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/#simplealphacompositing) · [WAI graphical objects](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) | The panel uses near-surface thumbnails on two fixed grounds. Check photographs with pale, dark, transparent, and already-distinct edges. A 10% decorative line does not establish a sufficient focus or selection indicator. |
| color-12 | A zero-blur, positive-spread outer shadow draws an outside edge. A second blurred shadow adds elevation. Forced colors suppresses box shadows, making a separate boundary necessary where that edge carries information. [CSS shadow layers](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#shadow-layers) · [CSS forced colors](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-adjust-1/#forced-colors-properties) | The two payment cards have an outer spread and blur versus plain borders. Correct the deep dive’s description of an inset shadow. Inspect the real control in forced colors when a card also conveys focus or selection. |

## Limits of the evidence

These comparisons establish what the source code paints or requests. They do not measure reading speed, comfort, errors, or conversions. For those questions, compare equivalent tasks with representative readers and record the font, script, theme, device, available width, and zoom level. Prioritize the smallest security detail, the two near-identical filenames, paragraph wrapping across engines, and the field edge under forced colors. Each exposes a different failure that a static desktop comparison can miss.
