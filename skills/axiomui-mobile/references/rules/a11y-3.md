# Leave space between touch targets

- ID: a11y-3
- Category: Accessibility & inclusivity
- Tags: Touch, Click targets, Spacing
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/a11y-3

Leave room between actions such as Cancel and Delete. Here, an 8px gap catches a tap 3px past Cancel. WCAG tests target size and spacing together.

- Do: 8px gap between buttons
- Don’t: 0px gap between buttons

## Why it works

Both buttons are 44px tall. A tap 3px past Cancel lands in the 8px gap. When the buttons touch, the same tap lands on Delete.

## What breaks

Toolbar icons can look well spaced while their hit areas overlap. Large adjacent buttons can be easy to tap without an 8px gap.

## How to apply it

1. Measure hit areas as well as glyphs.
2. If a target falls short of 24 × 24 CSS pixels, check the spacing exception in WCAG 2.5.8. Center a 24px-diameter circle on each undersized target’s bounding box. Unless another exception applies, each circle must avoid overlapping other targets and other undersized targets’ circles.
3. Separate actions that are costly to confuse, such as Cancel and Delete. Test them on the intended touchscreen.

## Before you ship

1. Where does a near miss land?
2. Do expanded hit areas overlap?
3. Could a tap just past Cancel trigger Delete?
