# Offer a click or tap alternative to dragging

- ID: a11y-4
- Category: Accessibility & inclusivity
- Tags: Accessibility, WCAG, Gestures, Touch
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/a11y-4

Let people click or tap to reorder a list, move a card between columns or set a slider value. Several taps can replace one drag.

- Do: Drag handle + Move up / Move down
- Don’t: Drag handle only

## Why it works

One tap on Move up moves Book room one place. Someone who can tap but struggles to hold and drag can still reorder the list.

- [WCAG 2.2 · Dragging movements](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html)
- [WCAG technique G219 · Alternatives to dragging](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G219)
- [WCAG 2.2 · Pointer gestures](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pointer-gestures.html)
- [Atlassian · Accessible drag and drop](https://atlassian.design/components/pragmatic-drag-and-drop/accessibility-guidelines)
- [Apple HIG · Drag and drop](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/drag-and-drop)

## What breaks

Someone who can tap a task but can’t hold it steady while dragging may release it into the wrong position or leave the list unchanged.

## How to apply it

1. Search for draggable, onDragEnd and pointer handlers that change positions. For each outcome, trace a click or tap path to the same update.
2. Use Move up and Move down for a list, a destination menu for a board, or step buttons or a value field for a slider. A sequence of individual clicks or taps can reach the same outcome as one drag.
3. WCAG 2.5.7 allows exceptions for essential dragging and drag behavior the browser provides unchanged. Check keyboard access separately under sys-8.

## Before you ship

1. In your reorderable list, tap Move up once on the item in position 2. Does it move to position 1?
2. Can taps reach every destination or value the drag can reach?
3. Do both input paths produce the same saved order or value?
