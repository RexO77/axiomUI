# Separate competing touch targets

- ID: a11y-3
- Category: Accessibility & inclusivity
- Tags: Touch, Click targets, Spacing
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/a11y-3

Leave space between nearby actions when an unintended tap would be costly. An 8px gutter protects the particular miss shown here. WCAG sets size and spacing tests rather than one minimum gap for every button.

- Do: Buttons: 8px gutter
- Don't: Buttons: 0px gutter

## Why it works

The preview places a tap three pixels past Cancel. With an eight-pixel gap it lands on neither button. With adjacent buttons it lands on Delete. Both targets are already 44px high, so the example illustrates error tolerance beyond simply meeting a size criterion.

- [WCAG 2.2 · Target spacing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html#spacing)

## What breaks

A toolbar can show ample space between visible glyphs while their invisible hit boxes overlap. Conversely, adjacent large segments can be usable without an eight-pixel gap.

## How to apply it

1. Measure hit areas as well as glyphs.
2. For targets below 24 × 24 CSS px, apply the WCAG 2.5.8 spacing test or its other exceptions.
3. Separate high-cost competing actions and try the arrangement on the intended touchscreen.

## Review questions

1. Where does a near miss land?
2. Do expanded hit areas overlap?
3. Is the protection appropriate to the cost of choosing the wrong action?
