# Keep keyboard focus visible

- ID: a11y-1
- Category: Accessibility & inclusivity
- Tags: Keyboard, WCAG, Accessibility
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/a11y-1

Keep a visible indicator on keyboard focus. A custom focus treatment should remain identifiable across the surfaces and display modes where the control appears.

- Do: focus-visible:ring-2 ring-blue-500
- Don't: outline: none with no replacement

## Why it works

A person tabbing across a billing dialog needs to tell whether the next activation will choose Cancel or Delete. The browser’s native indication can do that. Replacing it makes the product responsible for preserving that information in its custom colors and high-contrast modes.

- [WCAG 2.2 · Focus visible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-visible.html)
- [WCAG 2.2 · Focus not obscured (minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html)
- [WCAG 2.2 · Focus appearance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html)

## What breaks

A ring with sufficient contrast on white can disappear against a nearby fill or be clipped by an overflow container. A box shadow alone may also disappear in forced-colors mode.

## How to apply it

1. Keep the native outline or supply an equally discernible indicator.
2. Check author-styled focus against the relevant adjacent colors and test forced colors.
3. Tab past sticky headers and footers in both directions. WCAG 2.4.11 at AA also addresses focused components entirely hidden by author-created content.
4. Use :focus-visible where appropriate and respect the browser’s input heuristics, including text controls focused by pointer.

## Review questions

1. Can someone locate focus throughout the keyboard path?
2. Is the indicator distinguishable wherever it is drawn?
3. Does it remain visible under zoom, clipping and forced colors?
