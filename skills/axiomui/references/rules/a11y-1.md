# Keep keyboard focus visible

- ID: a11y-1
- Category: Accessibility & inclusivity
- Tags: Keyboard, WCAG, Accessibility
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/a11y-1

Show which control has keyboard focus. Check that a custom focus indicator stays visible on every background and in high-contrast modes.

- Do: focus-visible:outline-2 outline-offset-2
- Don’t: outline: none with no replacement

## Why it works

The outline on Save changes shows which button Enter will activate. If you replace the browser’s outline, check yours on every background and in high-contrast modes.

- [WCAG technique C40 · Two-color focus indicator](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/css/C40.html)
- [WCAG 2.2 · Focus appearance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html)

- [GOV.UK · Focus state styles](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/get-started/focus-states/)

## What breaks

A focus ring can disappear against a colored background or be clipped by a parent with overflow: hidden. Forced-colors mode can also hide a ring drawn with box-shadow alone.

## How to apply it

1. Keep the browser’s focus outline or replace it with one that’s just as easy to see.
2. Check a custom focus ring against every color it touches and in forced-colors mode.
3. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move past sticky headers and footers. Check that neither completely hides the focused control (WCAG 2.4.11, level AA).
4. Use :focus-visible to follow the browser’s focus cues. Clicking a text field can show the focus ring too.

## Before you ship

1. As you tab through the page, can you always see which control has focus?
2. Is the indicator distinguishable wherever it is drawn?
3. Does it remain visible under zoom, clipping and forced colors?
