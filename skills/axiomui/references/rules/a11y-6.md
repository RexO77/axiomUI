# Let people pause what moves or updates on its own

- ID: a11y-6
- Category: Accessibility & inclusivity
- Tags: Accessibility, WCAG, Motion
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/a11y-6

If content moves, blinks or scrolls automatically for over 5 seconds beside other content, let readers pause, stop or hide it unless the movement is essential. A rotating quote needs a control at any interval. Let readers pause it or choose how often it changes.

- Do: Rotating quote with a Pause button
- Don’t: Quote changes every 4s · no control

## Why it works

The testimonial strip replaces the quote while someone is reading the shared-calendar instructions just below it. Pause holds the current quote so the person can finish either passage at their own pace.

- [WCAG 2.2 · Pause, stop, hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html)
- [WAI-ARIA APG · Carousel pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/)
- [Baymard · Homepage carousels](https://baymard.com/research-articles/homepage-carousel)
- [W3C WAI · Carousel animation controls](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/carousels/animations/#add-playstop-button)

## What breaks

A quote can change before someone finishes reading it. Each change can also distract them from the calendar instructions.

## How to apply it

1. Search for autoplay, setInterval and polling callbacks in carousels, tickers and live feeds. Follow each start path and check which user control can stop the visible change.
2. Put a visible, keyboard-operable Pause control with the strip and show whether rotation is paused. Keep it paused until the reader resumes it.
3. For nonessential content that starts automatically alongside other content, WCAG 2.2.2 sets two tests. Movement, blinking or scrolling lasting over 5 seconds needs a way to pause, stop or hide it. Automatic updates need one of those controls at any interval, or a way to change the frequency.

## Before you ship

1. In your own rotating strip, tab to Pause and press Space. With a 4-second interval, does the same quote stay visible for two intervals?
2. Does the strip stay paused when focus leaves the control?
3. Can someone tell that the strip is paused before resuming it?
