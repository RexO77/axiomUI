# Bring repeated actions within reach

- ID: mobile-4
- Category: Mobile
- Tags: One-handed use, Reach
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/mobile-4

In a one-handed task, moving a frequently used action lower can reduce the stretch. The comfortable area changes with grip, hand and device size.

- Do: Save falls inside the selected reach arc
- Don’t: Top-corner Save falls outside the same arc

## Why it works

Placing a repeated action within the current grip saves a stretch on every use.

## What breaks

A top-corner Save makes someone shift their grip to finish each edit.

## How to apply it

1. iOS — Keep standard navigation controls in familiar positions. Test the placement of a frequent task action with either hand.
2. Android — For a primary floating action, account for the gesture bar and the hand reaching across the screen.
3. Web — Use logical alignment for language direction. Offer handedness only as a control in the teaching model.

## Before you ship

1. Does Save stay reachable with either hand?
2. Move the thumb pivot and switch hands. Does the center of Save still sit inside the arc?
3. Can someone complete the task on the larger screen?
