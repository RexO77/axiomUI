# Show the product doing what the headline promises

- ID: comp-15
- Category: Components & actions
- Tags: Hierarchy, Images, Marketing
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/comp-15

Show the screen or output that delivers the headline’s claim. Crop it so the result is easy to see at the size the image appears.

- Do: Headline + deploy #418 flagged in the product
- Don’t: Headline + floating avatars and a green check

## Why it works

The deploy list flags #418 beside a 38% rise in checkout errors. That row shows how the product helps someone find the deploy linked to the increase. In Nielsen Norman Group’s eye-tracking studies, people examined informative images and often passed over decorative ones.

- [Baymard · Showing the SaaS interface](https://baymard.com/research-articles/highlight-saas-ui#1-images)
- [NN/g · Photos as web content](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/photos-as-web-content/)
- [W3C WAI · Informative images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/informative/)
- [Baymard · Product images with descriptive text](https://baymard.com/research-articles/product-images-descriptive-text)

## What breaks

The headline promises to identify the deploy linked to checkout errors. The floating avatars and green check show neither the deploy nor the error.

## How to apply it

1. Choose the frame from the headline: the screen and state where the promised result is visible. Use real or realistic data, then crop and enlarge until the evidence is readable at the size the image ships.
2. Keep the proof legible without hover or animation. Mark the result with the product’s own highlight, and write alt text that states what the image shows, such as “Deploy #418 flagged beside a 38% rise in checkout errors”.
3. For each product claim, find the detail in the image that supports it. If it appears only in the image, describe it in the alt text.

## Before you ship

1. Cover the headline. What does the image alone show the product doing?
2. Swap a competitor’s name into the headline. Does the image still fit? If so, it proves nothing.
3. At the smallest display size, can readers identify the flagged deploy and read the change in checkout errors?
