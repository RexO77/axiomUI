# Prioritize the next action

- ID: comp-1
- Category: Components & actions
- Tags: Buttons, Hierarchy
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/comp-1

Give the next step in a task the strongest button treatment. When two choices deserve equal consideration, give them equal weight.

- Do: 1 filled + 1 outline + 1 ghost button
- Don't: 3 filled buttons side by side

## Why it works

In a project editor, Save, Cancel and Preview sit together but do different jobs. Giving Save a distinct treatment helps someone who has finished editing find their way out. A screen can contain several independent tasks with their own action groups.

- [GOV.UK · Button emphasis and usage](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/button/)

## What breaks

A filled “Accept” next to a faint “Decline” can steer a decision about consent. Equal prominence is appropriate when the choice belongs to the person making it.

## How to apply it

1. Group controls by the task they affect.
2. Use one prominent button within a group that has a clear next step.
3. Check that secondary actions remain legible, reachable and honestly labeled.

## Review questions

1. What is someone trying to finish here?
2. Can someone who wants to cancel find that option?
3. Is there a reason to recommend one of these choices?
