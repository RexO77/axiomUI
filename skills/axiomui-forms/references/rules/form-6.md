# Show options people need to compare

- ID: form-6
- Category: Forms & inputs
- Tags: Efficiency, Forms
- URL: https://axiomui.nischal.fyi/rules/form-6

Show short lists in full so people can compare options. Use a dropdown for a long list when people already know their answer, such as their country.

- Do: Three billing cycles visible together
- Don’t: Closed select hides the billing cycles

## Why it works

Showing all three billing cycles lets people compare them before choosing. With four or five options, consider which differences people need to see together.

## What breaks

A closed dropdown can hide a price difference. Dozens of radio buttons can make a familiar answer, such as a country, slow to find.

## How to apply it

1. Show options together when there is room for the details people need to compare, such as prices.
2. Use a native dropdown when space is tight and people know which option they want. For long lists, consider adding search.
3. Preserve keyboard use, focus visibility and a clear group label.

## Before you ship

1. Can someone compare the important differences before choosing?
2. Does a long list support finding a known answer?
3. Can the choice be made on a phone and with a keyboard?
